Redstone designs you can check, see and build

Dustwright describes a Minecraft Bedrock redstone circuit as one JSON file. It checks the design, simulates its truth table, shows it in 3D layer by layer, and exports it as a function, a structure or a behavior pack you import with a double-click. Everything runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

  • Playground: edit a design and watch the diagnostics and the 3D view.
  • Viewer: open a share link, a design URL or a file.
  • Patterns: working designs to start from.
  • Docs: how to write a design, the diagnostics and the Bedrock mapping.
